## ELIZA
I was emptily searching for chatbots and I searched for the first chatbot expecting chatGPT to pop up. Some one has already created on even before the websites. TBH, the world wide web is truly a web which catches you in the blink of an eye. I mean theoritically chatbots are just computer programs that convert numbers back into texts or words. But this specific program used pattern matching to produced feigned intent understanding. I think it was an MIT professor, named Jospeh Weizenbaum who developed this and called it Eliza - mimicking a Rogerian psychotherapist. 
The mechanism was simple. I saw lot of images that quite take the words from the user and checks a pre defined database filled with sentences and matches them accordingly. For questions that are not understood the replied were generic or it just repeated the question, suffixing it with terms like "interesting", "I understand" etcetera. But it was a good initiative ICL. Search it up yourself, don't be lazy. I can't provide the link.
