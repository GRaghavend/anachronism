## WHY AI INTERESTS ME? 
To be frank, I am not sure! I'll tell you how I got into AI.

My high school examinations were done and results had come by. I had decent marks, I think and I applied to a few colleges in and around TN. I eventually got into SASTRA and was allotted ECE - Cyber Physical Systems or something similar. I wasn't very happy since I wanted pure Computer Science.

At SASTRA, we have a rolling allotment system where, if someone leaves a seat vacant, it is allotted to the next deserving candidate. During the next round of allotment, I was directly moved to B.Tech CSE with AI & DS. Something told me to stop the allotment process. I'm still not sure whether this was meant to be or not, but things are okay right now.

There's a key reason why I like AI. It's a necessary evil, to be honest. I'm pretty lazy, and if something makes life simpler, maybe AI fills that void. Imagine yourself as a developer. You click icons and navigate through applications, and then you are introduced to Alt+Tab. Isn't it funny that AI is kind of the Alt+Tab of modern-day applications?

I just like the idea of building something, training it, scolding it, and making it work. Maybe that's why I like AI.

## WILL AI EAT ME OVER?
I am 100 percent confident in saying that AI will take over humans in at least a few ways. I think humanity is something it can't replace, hehe! But automation and orchestration are all around the corner.

That being said, I am once again not loyal to AI. It's pretty much a quid pro quo. If it eats me up, I eat it back!
